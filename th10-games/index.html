文法重點 Grammar

1. Indirect Speech 間接引語“I'm hurrying,” Becky said. → Becky said (that) she was hurrying.
“Can you help me?” he asked. → He asked if / whether I could help him.
2. take place / last fortake place＝什麼時候發生；last for＝持續多久
The game will take place on Monday. Dinner lasts for two hours.
3. could / might / must becould／might＋if＝可能會；must be＝一定是（最好的猜測）
The police officer must be going to the traffic accident.
4. Phrasal Verbsfall asleep 睡著・fall apart 散掉・fall open 打開掉出・fall over 倒下・fall out 掉出來
